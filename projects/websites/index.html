01Project Overview

I work on website development and customization, focusing on responsive layouts, landing pages, page-builder implementations, visual consistency, and practical user experience improvements.

02Business Problem

A business website is often the first place a customer forms an impression. It needs to explain the offer clearly, work well on every device, and make it easy to get in touch — and it needs to stay that way as content changes.

03Goals

  1. Clear structureOrganise content so visitors find what they need quickly.
  2. Responsive by defaultLayouts that work on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  3. Consistent visualsSpacing, typography, and components that feel like one brand.
  4. Easy to maintainPages and components that are straightforward to update.
